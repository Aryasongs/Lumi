Privacy Policy

Last updated — September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexor Sparks ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use Lumi (the "App"). Lumi is powered by KashishAI, a product of Nexor Sparks. When you create an account, we ask you to actively confirm — via a checkbox that isn't pre-selected for you — that you've read and consent to this Privacy Policy. If you don't consent, please don't create an account or use the App.

2. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

  • Account information — the email address and password you use to sign in (sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication), and any details you add to your profile in the app.
  • Profile information — profile photo, display name, and any other details you choose to add.
  • Content you create — messages, voice interactions, and any images you generate or upload while chatting with Lumi.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device information — device type, operating system, and app version, used for compatibility and bug fixing.
  • Usage data — which features you use and how often, to help us understand what's working and what isn't.
  • Approximate technical data such as IP-based region, used for security and abuse prevention.
  • Push notification tokens — if you allow notifications, we store a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so reminders and updates you've set up can reach you even when the App is closed. You can turn this off anytime from your device settings or the App's notification settings.

c) Information from third parties

If you sign in using a third-party provider, we receive basic profile information (such as name and email) from that provider, as permitted by your settings with them.

2A. Health & wellness features

Where available, Lumi includes optional Health features (water, mood, steps, sleep, weight/BMI, resting heart rate, workouts, meals, and similar logs). This section explains specifically how that data is handled, in addition to the rest of this Policy:

  • Stored on your device only. Everything you log in the Health screen is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not upload, sync, or store this health data on our servers, and we do not access it — unless you choose to share it with Lumi yourself, for example by tapping "Generate report," which sends the numbers already shown on your Health screen to the AI in that one conversation so it can write you a summary.
  • Device sensors. With your permission, step tracking uses your device's motion sensor, and air quality / UV index checks use your device's location, only while you actively use those features. You can decline or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings, and the related feature will simply show no data rather than a fake reading.
  • Third-party data lookups. Air quality and UV index use Open-Meteo; meal search uses Open Food Facts; workout search uses wger.de; medicine information uses the U.S. FDA's openFDA Drug Label API. These are free, independent public services — when you search or check something, your query and approximate location/IP may be sent directly to that provider (not stored by us) to return real results. Each operates under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • Not medical data or advice. Health screen figures (including calorie, MET-based, and similar estimates) are self-reported or sensor-derived approximations for your own reference, not clinical measurements, a diagnosis, or medical advice, and medicine information shown is official FDA label text for general reference only. See the Consumer Terms for more on this.

2B. Region check and the wishlist

Lumi is currently open only in certain countries. To work out where you are, this site checks the approximate country of your connection when the page loads. It first asks the network this site is served through. If that is not available, it may ask a public IP-lookup service (ipwho.is or ipapi.co), which receives your IP address in order to answer. As a last hint, it may read your device's time zone locally. We do not keep the result of this check unless you join the wishlist.

If you are outside an available country and choose Join the wishlist, we save your account ID, email address, country code, the time you joined, and a source tag in our database. We use them only to email you when Lumi opens in your country. You can ask us to remove your wishlist entry at any time at help@kashishai.com.

  • To create and maintain your account, and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To let Lumi remember conversation history and context so replies feel personal and continuous.
  • To operate core features, including text chat, voice, and image generation.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to abuse, fraud, security incidents, and violations of our Terms or Community Guidelines.
  • To fix bugs, improve reliability, and understand how the App is used so we can improve it.
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

4. How your data is processed

Your messages are sent to the AI systems that power Lumi's responses, along with recent conversation history, so the app can generate a relevant reply. This processing happens through infrastructure operated by Nexor Sparks and its service providers.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these situations:

  • With service providers who help us operate the App — such as hosting, authentication, database, and AI-processing providers — strictly to perform services on our behalf.
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Nexor Sparks, our users, or the public.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent, for any other purpose we disclose to you at the time.

6. Data retention

We retain your account information and conversation history for as long as your account is active, so Lumi can maintain context and history. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain certain data for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including authentication and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Children and minors

Lumi is not intended for children under the minimum age required in their region, and by creating an account you confirm that you meet that age requirement. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age, and we do not independently verify age. If we learn that we've collected information from someone below the required age, we will take steps to delete it.

  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, by emailing help@kashishai.com or contacting support through the app.
  • You can withdraw consent you previously gave (for example, during signup) at any time by emailing help@kashishai.com or contacting support through the app; this doesn't affect the lawfulness of anything we did based on your consent before you withdrew it, and may mean we can no longer provide parts of the App that depend on it.

Cookies and Storage

Last updated — September 2026

This page explains what Lumi stores in your browser and which outside services your browser talks to when you use this site.

What we store in your browser

  • Sign-in session. When you log in, Firebase Authentication keeps a token in your browser's storage (local storage or IndexedDB) so you stay signed in. Logging out removes it.
  • Wellness logs. If you use the optional wellness features, your logs are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. See section 2A of the Privacy Policy.

What we do not use

This site does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics trackers.

Outside services your browser contacts

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) loads the Fredoka and Figtree typefaces. Google receives your IP address and browser details when it serves them.
  • Firebase (Google) handles sign-in and stores wishlist entries.
  • Country check. The site asks the network it is served through for your approximate country. If that fails, it may call ipwho.is or ipapi.co, which see your IP address. Your device's time zone may be read locally as a last hint. We only save your country if you join the wishlist.

Your choices

  • Log out from the site to remove your sign-in session.
  • Clear this site's data from your browser settings at any time.
  • Block third-party requests with a browser extension. Sign-in and the country check may stop working if you do.
